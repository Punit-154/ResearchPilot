What do "citation" and "verification status" mean?

Every factual sentence the system generates (a claim) must point to a specific evidence passage from the paper it's based on (a citation, e.g. [E01]). We don't just trust that the citation is honest — a separate AI model independently checks whether the cited text actually supports the claim.

  • VALID — independently confirmed: the cited evidence directly supports this claim.
  • FLAGGED — could not be confirmed automatically. This does not mean the claim is false — often it's true but phrased in a way (e.g. combining facts from multiple sentences) the automated checker couldn't verify with confidence. It means: worth a human double-check.
  • NO_CITATIONS — the system made a claim without citing any evidence (should be rare).
